File limits and how it works

Each file can be up to 3 hours long and 1 GB in size, or less if you have fewer MB left. Ready files stay here for 24 hours. Save them on your phone before then.

Only files we finish preparing count toward your limit. Failed attempts and saving the same ready file again do not count twice.

A free trial allows up to 10 attempts a day. The monthly plan allows 60. We cannot unlock protected videos or sign in to private video accounts.

        Good to know

        Do I need to sign in or connect every time?

        Sign in by email once in each browser. Your browser can remember you for up to 90 days. If you switch browsers or your sign-in expires, ask for a new email link.

        Connect Offline Vault once on each phone or tablet. The app remembers the connection for up to 90 days. Open Transfer for new files.

        Is the app free?

        Yes. Offline Vault for iPhone and iPad is free. Cloud Drop is an optional service that gets files ready for the app.

        What does 5 GB mean?

        It is the total size of files you can prepare in one month. A longer or sharper video usually uses more. Choosing Audio or 720p video helps your GB go further.

        Does this save to my phone by itself?

        There is one last step: open Transfer in Offline Vault and tap the download arrow. When it finishes, your file is in Library.

        Do my saved files disappear?

        No. Files you have saved in the app stay on your phone, even if you stop your Cloud Drop plan. Files waiting on this website are removed after 24 hours.
