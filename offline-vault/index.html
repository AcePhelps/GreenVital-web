Files already saved on your phone stay available after you stop sharing.

  1. Get both apps

    Install Offline Vault on your iPhone or iPad and the companion on your Mac or Windows computer.

  2. Start sharing

    Use the same Wi-Fi on both devices. On your computer, choose a media folder and select Start Sharing.

  3. Scan the code

    On your phone, open Settings, choose Connect Your Computer, and scan the code on your computer. If already connected, choose Connect Another Computer.

  4. Save and play

    Open Transfer, tap a file’s download arrow, and wait for it to finish. Open Library to play your saved file offline.

A little help getting started.

Do I need a computer?

You can import compatible audio and video from Files on your phone. A companion is needed when transferring directly from your own computer.

Why can’t my phone find my computer?

Check that both devices use the same Wi-Fi, sharing is running, and Offline Vault has Local Network permission in iPhone Settings. A guest network or VPN may prevent a connection.

Do I have to pay for the app?

No. Offline Vault for iPhone and iPad and Offline Vault Companion for Mac are free downloads. Cloud Drop is a separate, optional service.
