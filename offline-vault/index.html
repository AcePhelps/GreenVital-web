Good to know

Does Offline Vault require a subscription?

No. The planned price is $19.99 as a one-time purchase.

Is the computer companion included?

Yes. The Offline Vault Companion for macOS and Windows is included at no extra cost.

What does the Windows companion require?

The prepared release supports Windows 10 version 1809 or newer and Windows 11. It is self-signed, so users must verify and install the GreenVital public certificate before opening App Installer.

Do my iPhone and computer need the same Wi-Fi?

Yes. Keep both devices on the same local network while pairing and transferring selected files.

Can I import files without the companion?

Yes. Compatible audio and video files can also be imported from Files on iPhone.

Is a hosted service available?

Not currently. A hosted option may be considered later, but no availability or price has been announced.
