Cloud Drop

Works with Offline Vault

A video link. A file to keep offline.

Paste a video link and choose video or audio. Cloud Drop prepares the file. You then save it in the free Offline Vault app.

Try 2 files free →

Up to 200 MB in total, once per account. No card. No automatic subscription. Paid subscriptions are not open yet.

  1. Get the app and sign in

    Install free Offline Vault on your iPhone or iPad. Sign in to Cloud Drop with your email.

  2. Connect inside the app

    Show Cloud Drop’s code on another screen. In Offline Vault, open Settings → Connect Your Computer and scan the code.

  3. Prepare here. Save in the app.

    Paste a link into Cloud Drop. When your file is ready, open Transfer in the app and tap the download arrow. Play it offline from Library.

First time? You need your iPhone or iPad and another screen, such as a computer or tablet, for the first connection.
