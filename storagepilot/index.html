Local files stay local

Your media is not the ad product.

StoragePilot does not upload the files it scans or the outputs it creates. The free version does use Google AdMob, which handles limited advertising, analytics, diagnostic, and fraud-prevention data separately from your media.

How data is handled
  • Photos, videos, filenames, thumbnails, paths, and scan results stay on your device.
  • Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and ad or app interactions.
  • Google's User Messaging Platform provides consent and privacy choices where required.
  • After Pro entitlement confirmation, StoragePilot makes no ad requests.
