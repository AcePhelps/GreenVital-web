Urgent pet situations

PetShift is not a veterinary service, emergency response service, or pet monitoring service. For urgent pet health or safety issues, contact the pet owner, veterinarian, local emergency veterinary clinic, or local emergency services as appropriate.

Contact support

Email support@greenvital.app. Include your account email, device type, app version if known, and a short description of the issue.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, private access codes, or other sensitive credentials.

What we can help with

  • Sign-in problems and account access.
  • Invitation links, owner/sitter access, and private stay coordination.
  • Stay setup, care tasks, completion history, and report questions.
  • Report notes, uploaded photos, pet avatars, and care details.
  • Push notifications and device notification settings.
  • Privacy questions, data access questions, and deletion requests.
  • Bug reports and security concerns.

Helpful details to include

  • The email address used for your PetShift account.
  • Whether you are using PetShift as an owner or sitter.
  • The general issue category, such as sign-in, invite, stay, report, photo, notification, privacy, or deletion.
  • Your device type and operating system version if known.
  • Screenshots if they help explain the issue and do not expose sensitive information.
