PetShift

A clear care plan for your pet.

Share your pet’s routine with a sitter you trust. They see what needs doing. You see updates, photos, and a record of care.

From your routine to their checklist.

  1. Add your pet

    Save care instructions, routines, preferences, and emergency contacts.

  2. Plan the stay

    Choose the dates and adjust care times for this stay.

  3. Invite your sitter

    Send a private invitation to someone you trust.

  4. Follow along

    See tasks, notes, photos, and care summaries as your sitter adds them.

A clear view for each of you.

For pet owners

Build the plan once. Adjust meals, walks, and other tasks for each stay. See progress and stay in touch with your sitter.

For pet sitters

See today’s tasks and important contacts. Mark tasks complete, skipped, or cancelled, and add a note or photo when helpful.

See the care flow.

Private care, with clear limits.

Invitations give your sitter access to the care information for their assigned stay. PetShift does not find or book sitters, take payments, track GPS location, or independently verify that care happened.

For an urgent pet health or safety concern, contact the owner, a veterinarian, or local emergency help directly.
