What PetShift is, and what it is not.

Clear product boundaries help owners and sitters use PetShift for the right jobs and use direct human help when it matters.

Is PetShift a sitter marketplace?

No. PetShift is for private coordination between owners and sitters who already know each other.

Does PetShift handle booking or payment?

No. It is not a booking, payment, review, or public discovery platform.

Does it guarantee notifications?

No. Notifications depend on device settings, networks, and platform delivery. Critical care should not rely only on push notifications.

Is it for veterinary or emergency response?

No. For urgent pet health or safety issues, contact the owner, veterinarian, emergency clinic, or local emergency services.
