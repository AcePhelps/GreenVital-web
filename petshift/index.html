Safety & privacy

Built for private care handoff, not public sitter discovery.

Private by default

PetShift is invitation-based for owners and sitters who already know each other.

Access only for the stay

Owner and sitter access is scoped to the stay, pet, and care information they are allowed to see.

Clear local timing

Live schedules show timing in the sitter’s local zone to reduce handoff confusion.

Care history stays intact

Activity history is designed to preserve a care record instead of rewriting what happened.

Direct help when needed

PetShift keeps owner, veterinary, and emergency contact paths visible when real-world help matters.

PetShift is for private coordination. It is not a marketplace, booking or payment service, public review platform, veterinary service, emergency response service, GPS tracker, or guarantee that every notification will be delivered.
