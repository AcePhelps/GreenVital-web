How to opt in

When GreenVital invites you to the pilot and confirms it is ready, text START to 877-788-0333 from your registered test phone. Joining the text pilot is optional. GreenVital records your opt-in before sending pet-care updates.

You may receive care status updates and photos made with test data. You can reply to the same number, and your reply will appear in the test PetShift inbox.

Stop messages or get help

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You can also email support@greenvital.app.
