Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of a PetShift account and how shared care history, active stays, backups, and verification may affect the process.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Privacy Delete account Support Terms

Current deletion status

The PetShift app currently records deletion requests, but irreversible account deletion is not yet release-ready. This page is published to provide a clear request path and to avoid overstating the current product capability.

Request deletion in the app

In PetShift, go to Account > Privacy & data and submit an account deletion request. This records your request for review.

Deleting the app from your phone is not the same as deleting your account. Uninstalling removes the app from your device, but it does not necessarily remove account, stay, pet, invite, report, or shared care records from the service.

Request deletion without reinstalling the app

If you cannot access PetShift or do not want to reinstall the app, email support@greenvital.app with the subject PetShift Account Deletion Request.

Include the email address associated with your PetShift account and a short note that you are requesting account deletion. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or private access codes.

Verification and active-care blockers

We may need to verify your identity or authority before processing a request. If your account is involved in an active stay, unresolved invitation, owner-sitter handoff, safety issue, support investigation, or pet-care responsibility, deletion may need to be delayed, limited, or coordinated to protect care continuity and authorized participants.

Shared history and backups

Some PetShift information may be shared between authorized owners and sitters. Records such as stay history, reports, notes, and photos may need to remain visible to another authorized participant or be minimized rather than fully removed from every view immediately.

Backup copies may persist for a limited period as part of normal system operation. We do not claim immediate or automatic erasure from every backup or log.

Questions

Contact support@greenvital.app for privacy or deletion help.
